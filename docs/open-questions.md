# Open questions

Things that need an answer before the site can be finished. When a question
is answered, move it to **Answered** at the bottom with the answer, who gave
it, and the date. Don't delete it; the history is useful.

## For Mr. Pierson

### Branding

- **Team colors and logo.** The current palette is sampled from a 2015
  team-shirt photo and is a placeholder. Need the real color values and logo
  files (SVG preferred, or high-resolution PNG).
- **One school or two.** FIRST's 2026 registration lists only Liberty Senior
  High School. Earlier years (2018 through 2023) listed both Liberty High and
  Liberty North. Does the team draw from one school now? If it draws from
  both, the site should not use either school's colors or mascot.

### Content

- **Founding year.** The current site says 2005. FIRST and The Blue Alliance
  list 1764's rookie year as 2006, and the mockups use 2006. Was 2005 the
  year the club formed, before its first competition season?
- **Meeting location.** The current site says the Z Basement. Mockups A, B,
  and C say Room 415. Bumper leaves it blank. Which is right?
- **Sign-up link.** Every mockup has a "Sign up to visit" button that goes
  nowhere yet. The current site uses a Google Form. Is that still the form
  to use?
- **Student testimonials.** The "Robot builds me" quotes are placeholders.
  Need real quotes, with permission to publish each student's name and
  class year.
- **Photos and video.** What is the existing policy for publishing student
  photos, names, and video, and who signs off? The Bumper direction needs a
  10 to 15 second robot clip.
- **Season records.** The mockups show 2023 through 2026 with placeholder
  records. Supply results and awards for each season, or approve using the
  official FIRST and The Blue Alliance records.
- **Sponsors.** The mockups use an older sponsor list. FIRST's 2026
  registration lists the Liberty Robotics Foundation and KC STEM Alliance.
  Need the current list and permission to show sponsor logos.
- **Foundation.** Should the site link to the Liberty Robotics Foundation,
  or carry a donate or sponsor page? Rebuilding the foundation's own site is
  out of scope for this project.

### Students and GitHub

- **Student accounts.** Students will contribute through their own GitHub
  accounts. GitHub requires account holders to be at least 13. Does the
  district have a policy on students using outside accounts for school
  activities, and is parent permission needed?
- **Repository ownership.** The repo currently lives in a volunteer's
  personal account. It should move to a GitHub organization owned by the
  club so it survives volunteer and student turnover. Who should the
  organization owners be?

## For the district

- **Web server access.** The approved site deploys from this repo to the
  team's existing web server. Need to know what that server is, how it is
  accessed, and who administers it. The deploy automation depends on the
  answer.
- **Domain owner.** Nobody currently knows who controls `first1764.com`. A
  lapsed domain can be taken by someone else, so this is worth chasing
  regardless of this project.
- **District link.** Liberty High School's site links to Team 1764 under
  Student Life. Someone with Finalsite access has to update that link when
  the new site goes live.
- **Accessibility standard.** The district publishes a Web Accessibility
  page. Under the DOJ's ADA Title II rule, public entities' web content must
  meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, with compliance dates now in April 2027 or April
  2028 depending on size. Does the district treat the team site as its
  content, and is there a standard or review it must pass? The site is being
  built to WCAG 2.1 AA either way.
