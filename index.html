Liberty Robotics 1764

Design preview. Not the team's live website.

Four directions for a rebuilt Team 1764 site. All four show the same core content, so the difference you are judging is layout, typography, and tone rather than what the page says. Bumper also adds a kickoff countdown and a video slot, because that direction depends on them.

Nothing here is final. The colors are sampled from a team photograph and the records and quotes are placeholders.

The directions

  1. Archive

    Dark and dense. The season record is the main event, on the theory that a team that documents itself well is the most convincing thing a robotics team can show.

  2. Institutional

    Light and roomy, led by a photograph. Immediately legible to a parent, a sponsor, or a principal.

  3. Editorial

    Type-led and asymmetric, built around student voices. The most print-like of the four.

  4. Bumper

    Built for recruiting. The team number sits on a robot bumper, with tiles for video, the next kickoff, and how to join. Aimed at the student deciding whether to show up.
